# Proposed Supplemental Figure 1F legend and interpretation

**(F) SCT-normalized marker expression by refined TG neuronal identity.** Dot color gives the mean SCT assay `data` value across donor-supported nuclei in each refined cluster, and dot area gives the percentage of nuclei with a value greater than zero. The eight genes were selected to inspect the stated cLTMR, NF2-SC03, NF3-SC02, and NF1/NF2 marker descriptions. Cluster summaries are descriptive; the companion `s1f_refined_marker_expression_by_donor.csv` table reports donor-specific values. Values are not cross-gene measures of protein abundance or evidence of a cell-fate transition.

## Suggested response to the NF1 comment

In this GSE197289 reference, NF1 has higher **NTRK1** and **SCN10A** SCT-normalized expression than NF2 and NF3. Across all nuclei in each family, the NF1/NF2/NF3 means are 0.707/0.040/0.009 for NTRK1 and 0.629/0.018/0.006 for SCN10A. The ordering is the same in donors 1 and 2; donor 3 contributes only seven NF1 nuclei. NF1-SC02 has lower expression of both genes than NF1-SC01 and NF1-SC03, and several non-neurofilament families also express these genes strongly. The wording should therefore specify the **relative NF1 versus NF2/NF3 comparison within this reference**, rather than imply uniformly high expression in NF1 or a general property of all human NF1 neurons.

Suggested replacement sentence: “NF1 and NF2 were retained as family-level neurofilament-positive A-LTMR reference descriptors. Within this donor-derived reference, NF1 showed higher NTRK1 and SCN10A expression than NF2 and NF3, whereas NF2 showed higher NTRK3 expression.”
